# Outbreak Raiders — Privacy Policy

**Effective and last updated: October 8, 2026**

**HackerHouse** ("we", "us", or "the developer") operates **Outbreak Raiders** on
Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit). This policy explains how we handle
information for the App. Reddit separately handles information for its accounts,
hosting, and payment services under the
[Reddit Privacy Policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## 1. Information the App uses

We receive information from Reddit, your gameplay, your browser, and information
you provide when contacting support:

- **Reddit identity and community context:** your Reddit account ID, username,
  public avatar, and the community and post where the App runs. Account IDs can
  be linked to Reddit accounts and are not anonymous identifiers.
- **Game progress:** saves, raids and results, equipment, resource balances,
  upgrades, energy, missions, login rewards, event participation, tutorial and
  story completion, preferences, and game timestamps.
- **Rankings and social features:** scores, ranks, public game profiles, awards,
  frames, flair, historical ranking snapshots, and referral relationships used
  to credit game rewards. If you share a referral link, it includes a reference
  associated with your game account so the App can credit that referral.
- **Purchase records:** Reddit order IDs, the purchasing account, product IDs,
  prices in Gold, relevant checkout metadata such as the chosen season,
  delivery and refund status, and the benefits granted or removed. These records
  help deliver purchases, prevent duplicate delivery, and resolve disputes.
- **Usage and diagnostics:** visits, activity and economy counters, tutorial
  completion or skips, loading and transfer timings, error messages, App build,
  and available browser/device characteristics. These may include browser user
  agent, approximate device memory, processor concurrency, screen dimensions,
  pixel density, network type or speed estimates, and data-saving preference.
  Diagnostics can include your Reddit account ID and timestamps.
- **Support and administration:** messages and information you send us, player
  recovery checkpoints, gifts or purchase repairs, and audit records identifying
  administrative actions and the accounts involved.

The App does not receive your Reddit password or full payment-card or bank
details. Reddit and its payment providers handle payment credentials. We do not
ask for your real name, postal address, phone number, or precise location to play.
If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send.

## 2. Why we use it

We use this information to save and restore progress, deliver digital purchases,
handle refunds, calculate rewards and rankings, run community features, provide
support, prevent cheating and duplicate transactions, measure performance and
game activity, and fix errors. We also use necessary records to comply with legal
obligations and resolve disputes.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, these uses rely on providing
the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the
game, applicable legal obligations, or consent where required. The applicable
basis depends on the information and purpose.

## 3. Storage on Reddit and your device

The App's backend game records are stored in Reddit's managed Devvit
infrastructure, including its database and logs. Saves and purchases are keyed
to your Reddit account within the community's app installation.

The App also uses browser storage, including local storage and Unity/browser
save storage, for local progress, settings, tutorial flags, and cached assets.
Logged-out play can create local progress and diagnostics; signing in may
associate local progress with your account. Clearing browser storage can remove
local data but does not by itself delete records on Reddit's servers.

Support correspondence is handled through the email or Reddit service you use
to contact us. We do not operate a separate external gameplay database or add
third-party advertising or analytics trackers to the App. We do use the App's
own usage and diagnostic reporting described above. Reddit may independently
collect information under its own policy.

## 4. Who can see information

**Other players and Reddit viewers:** public rankings and player profiles can
show your username, Reddit avatar, selected raider, level, stars, scores, ranks,
and awards. The App can automatically publish community results, Hall of Fame
posts, and ranking flair. These public features do not depend on you choosing
to share a result. By contrast, posting a brag comment **as your account** is an
action you choose in the App. Public Reddit content is subject to Reddit's
visibility settings and can be copied by others.

**The developer and authorized administrators:** people with the relevant
developer or moderator permissions can access operational information.
Moderator economy tools show game activity and purchase totals. Detailed revenue
reports can include purchaser identifiers and purchase amounts; those reports
and player support tools are restricted to approved administrators who also
have moderator permissions.
Purchase ledgers and support records are not published on public leaderboards.

**Reddit and service providers:** Reddit processes information to host the App,
provide platform features, and operate payments. Email or Reddit support
services process correspondence sent through them. Reddit's and those
providers' own policies govern their independent handling of information.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We
may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate
abuse, protect users, or exercise legal rights, subject to applicable law and
Reddit's developer requirements.

## 5. Retention

Persistent saves, purchases, balances, and account history are retained while
needed to provide progress and entitlements, recover purchases, prevent abuse,
or meet legal obligations. Most persistent records do not currently have a
fixed automatic expiry. Stopping play or resetting progress is not the same as
deleting all account and purchase records.

Some temporary data expires or is replaced. For example, closed daily ranking
data and weekly ranking snapshots use limited retention periods, and the
diagnostic database keeps a rolling set of the latest 500 timing reports per
page type. Separate platform logs may retain the same events according to
Reddit's controls. Support checkpoints and audit records may remain after a
game reset. Public posts and comments remain on Reddit unless separately removed.
