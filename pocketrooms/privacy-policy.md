# Pocket Rooms — Privacy Policy

**Effective and last updated: November 23, 2026**

**HackerHouse** ("we", "us", or "the developer") operates **Pocket Rooms** (the
"App"), a daily escape-room game, on Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit). This
policy explains how we handle information for the App. Reddit separately handles
information for its accounts, hosting, and payment services under the
[Reddit Privacy Policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## 1. Information the App uses

We receive information from Reddit, your gameplay, your browser, and information
you provide when contacting support:

- **Reddit identity and community context:** your Reddit account ID, username,
  and the community and post where the App runs. Account IDs can be linked to
  Reddit accounts and are not anonymous identifiers.
- **Your coins:** your coin balance, the date of your first visit, the daily
  gifts you have collected, whether you have bought the Rookie Kit, and the end
  date of a Gold Pass.
- **Hints and rooms:** which hints you have paid for in each room (so reading
  them again is free), the time you first entered each room, and, the first time
  you escape a room, the coins it paid. That amount is worked out from the
  escape time your device reports.
- **Hide the Key rooms you make:** the room you chose, where you hid things,
  the clue you picked, the box code, the time you posted, and your username.
  Posting a room creates a public Reddit post under your account.
- **Hide the Key rooms you play:** the places you searched, in order, whether
  you found the key, and whether you posted your result as a comment. Each room
  also keeps a count of how many players searched it and how many found the key.
- **Purchase records:** Reddit order IDs, the purchasing account, the product
  bought, the coins and Gold Pass days delivered, the time of delivery, and
  refund status. These records help deliver purchases, prevent duplicate
  delivery, and resolve disputes.
- **Community standing checks:** before a Hide the Key room is posted under
  your name, the App asks Reddit whether your account is banned or muted in the
  community. The answer is used only to decide whether the post can be made
  and is not stored.
- **Diagnostics:** server error messages and request counters used to keep the
  game running and stop abuse. Error messages can include your username, Reddit
  order IDs, and timestamps.
- **Support:** messages and information you send us.

The App does not receive your Reddit password or full payment-card or bank
details. Reddit and its payment providers handle payment credentials. We do not
ask for your real name, postal address, phone number, or precise location to play.
If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send.

## 2. Why we use it

We use this information to keep your coins, charge and remember hints, pay coin
rewards, deliver purchases, handle refunds, run Hide the Key rooms between
players, provide support, prevent cheating and duplicate transactions, and fix
errors. We also use necessary records to comply with legal obligations and
resolve disputes.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, these uses rely on providing
the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the
game, applicable legal obligations, or consent where required. The applicable
basis depends on the information and purpose.

## 3. Storage on Reddit and your device

The App's server records are stored in Reddit's managed Devvit infrastructure,
including its database and logs. Coins, hints, purchases, and Hide the Key
records are keyed to your Reddit account within the community's app
installation.

Your progress inside a room is kept in your browser, not on our servers: the
puzzles you have solved, your timer, the items you hold, your Case Files, the
introduction and tips you have seen, your sound setting, and a Hide the Key room
you are still building. If you play without signing in, the free hints you have
used are also noted only in your browser. Clearing browser storage removes this
local data, and it does not delete records on Reddit's servers.

Support correspondence is handled through the email or Reddit service you use
to contact us. We do not operate a separate external database or add
third-party advertising or analytics trackers to the App, and the App makes no
network requests outside Reddit. Reddit may independently collect information
under its own policy.

## 4. Who can see information

**Other players and Reddit viewers:** a Hide the Key room is a public Reddit
post under your account, and players who open it see your username. If you have
a Gold Pass, a gold badge is shown next to your name there, which tells other
players that you have one. If you choose to post your result, it becomes a
public Reddit comment under your account. A room can show how many players
searched it and what share of them found the key, without naming them. These
features are part of how Hide the Key works. Your coin balance, hints, and
purchase records are not shown to other players.

**The developer:** we can read the App's server records and logs to provide
support, fix problems, and investigate abuse.

**Reddit and service providers:** Reddit processes information to host the App,
provide platform features, and operate payments. Email or Reddit support
services process correspondence sent through them. Reddit's and those
providers' own policies govern their independent handling of information.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We
may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate
abuse, protect users, or exercise legal rights, subject to applicable law and
Reddit's developer requirements.

## 5. Retention

Your coins, paid hints, room rewards, and purchase records are kept while the
game runs and while they are needed to provide your coins and entitlements,
recover purchases, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Stopping play is
not the same as deleting these records.

Some records expire or are removed on their own:

- The notes that you collected a day's gift, and the count of keys you found
  that day, expire after about two days.
- The counters that limit how many rooms can be posted expire within two days.
- When a Hide the Key post is deleted, the App deletes that room, every
  player's searches in it, and its counts. Reddit's own copy of a post or
  comment follows Reddit's rules.

Separate platform logs may keep events according to Reddit's controls.
