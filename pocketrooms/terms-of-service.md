## 2. Playing the game

In Pocket Rooms you are locked in a small 3D room and solve its puzzles to get
out. A new room is posted to the community regularly. **Every room is free to
play**, including older rooms; no purchase is needed to open, play, or finish
any room.

Your progress inside a room is saved in the browser you play in. Clearing your
browser data or changing devices can lose that progress, and it does not move
between devices. Your coins, the hints you have paid for, and your purchases are
associated with your Reddit account in the community's app installation; do not
assume they transfer between accounts or communities. If you play without
signing in, you have no coins: each room gives you one free room hint and one
free object hint.

Keep your account secure. Connectivity problems, clearing browser data, platform
changes, and removal of an installation can affect access to progress. Contact
us if you have a problem with your coins or a paid item.

## 3. Coins and hints

**Coins** are the App's in-game currency. Coins buy hints: a room hint from Iris
or an object hint in the item view, at the price shown before you confirm. A
hint you have paid for can be read again for free. Hints are optional; every
room can be solved without them.

You earn coins by playing, at the amounts the App shows: a welcome gift, a gift
on the first visit of each day, a reward the first time you escape each room
(more for a fast escape and for using no room hints), and Hide the Key rewards.
Each room pays its escape reward once per account. We may change future prices
and reward amounts as described in section 8.
